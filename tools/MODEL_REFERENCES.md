# Vehicle modelling references

Visual references only; photographs and drawings are not included in game assets.

## Electric locomotive

[Järnvägsmuseet, Rc1 1007](https://www.jarnvagsmuseet.se/utforska/vara-tag/ellok/ellok-rc1-1007)
([photograph](https://www.jarnvagsmuseet.se/siteassets/start-jvm/bilder/utforska/vara-tag/ellok/rc1-1007/rc1-1007-3.jpg)).

Compare paired framed windscreens and wipers, both driving cabs, corrugated
sides, round side windows, access doors and steps, bogie springs, roof
insulators and connected pantograph linkages. The game's red livery and
stylised proportions are retained; this is not an exact replica.

## Container ship

[Damen Container Feeder 800, photograph and general arrangement](https://medialibrary.damen.com/m/2be43bb54e194b64/original/specification-container-feeders-800.pdf).

Compare the flared bow, aft accommodation and wide wheelhouse, individual
bridge windows, deck railings and ladders, rescue-boat davits, mast/radar,
funnel, mooring equipment and corrugated stacked containers. Keep the
existing compact footprint so the ship fits the harbour scenery.

## Water warning sign

[Transportstyrelsen, A7 Varning för kaj](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/sv/vagtrafik/trafikregler-och-vagmarken/vagmarken/varningsmarken/varning-for-kaj/)
([reference image](https://www.transportstyrelsen.se/globalassets/global/vag/vagmarken2/a.-varningsmarken/a07.-varning-for-kaj/a7-1.png)).

Keep the Swedish rounded red triangle, yellow field, black quay edge and
two bold water ripples. Replace the car with an original flat snowracer
pictogram: saddle, open triangular frame, steering wheel and three skis,
tipping nose-first beyond the edge. Shares the bridge warning's plate.
